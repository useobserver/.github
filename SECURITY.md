# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the
[useobserver](https://github.com/useobserver) organization and the Observer
service at [use.observer](https://use.observer).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@use.observer**. Please do not open a public issue, pull
request, or discussion for a suspected vulnerability.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- The affected repository, release, or URL.
- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Any logs, requests, or screenshots that help confirm it.
- How you would like to be credited, if at all.

We read every report and follow up by email. Please give us a reasonable
chance to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Testing guidelines

- Only test against accounts and data you own.
- Do not degrade the service for other users, and do not run denial of
  service or automated high-volume scans.
- Do not access, modify, or keep data that is not yours. If you reach
  someone else's data by accident, stop and include that in your report.
